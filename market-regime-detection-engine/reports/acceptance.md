# MVP acceptance evidence

## Run and findings

The fixture contains 455 synthetic weekday closes with five-minute publication latency. The training cutoff of 2023-12-29 23:59:59 UTC admitted 260 bars and 245 complete feature vectors. All 195 later bars form the holdout, from 2024-01-01 to 2024-09-27. One missing price and a 25-bar synthetic liquidity/volatility shock exercise recovery. The raw evaluation identifies the exact input, settings and fitted model. [Evaluation](evaluation.json) · [Fixture manifest](../data/fixtures/manifest.json)

The HMM published a known label on 153 of 195 bars (78.5%) and changed label 10 times (5.15 per 100 adjacent intervals). The rule published a known label on 139 bars (71.3%) and changed label 11 times (5.67 per 100). The HMM made three changes directly between known states. The rule made all its changes through unknown. Median uninterrupted runs, including unknown, lasted 18 and 12 bars respectively.

The HMM abstained on 17 out-of-support observations. Three of ten chronological blocks had at least one feature whose mean moved over two training standard deviations. Each block reports its arrival range and complete count. These descriptive thresholds are sensitive to block size and overlapping windows.

The illustrative allocation targets long exposure only while rising and cash otherwise. The latest signal published strictly before a valid close may change exposure at that close. Missing prices defer valuation and trading until the next valid mark. The comparison starts in cash, charges 6 basis points for each one-way position change, and liquidates at the last observed mark. The long-only benchmark enters at its first eligible close under the same timing and fee rules. The HMM simulation returned +0.39% net against +0.62% for the rule and −35.26% for long-only. Greater HMM label coverage did not translate into a higher simulated return than the rule. The raw report gives gross results, fees, turnover and the range of nonoverlapping ten-mark mean returns. A single synthetic path cannot estimate deployment performance or regime accuracy.

## Evidence by requirement

- **M1, causal features and domain invariants.** Unit tests independently compute all four features and exercise event ordering, availability, gaps, invalid values and incomplete windows. The fixture generator and manifest reproduce the source bytes. [Unit tests](../tests/unit/test_core.py) · [Contracts](../docs/contracts.md)
- **M2, training-only estimation.** Scenario tests check frozen centering and fitted parameters, reject a late-published training event, and validate the transition matrix and score normalization. Truncating the input file changes its identity while preserving the trained model and all overlapping decisions. [Scenarios](../tests/scenarios/test_replay.py) · [Replay integration tests](../tests/integration/test_service.py)
- **M3, uncertainty and stability.** The noisy-threshold scenario holds the published label through alternating confident candidates and changes it after three confirmations. Low confidence, missing data, structural shift and cold start exercise abstention and recovery. Future changes leave the emitted prefix identical. [Scenarios](../tests/scenarios/test_replay.py)
- **M3, history and provenance.** Integration tests exercise as-of reads, exclusive cursor pagination, actual HTTP requests, credential rejection, malformed queries, atomic rollback, concurrent retries and exact history reproduction. [Integration tests](../tests/integration/test_service.py)
- **M4, comparative evaluation.** The report includes transition frequency, coverage, block drift and both candidate strategies alongside long-only. A hand-calculated cost test covers a delayed signal, an unpriced close and round-trip charges. [Evaluation](evaluation.json) · [Cost test](../tests/unit/test_core.py)
- **Reproduction and operations.** Raw local validation output and a five-repetition timing sample record the environment and outcomes. Timing measures in-memory inference only. Setup and demo run from a fresh checkout using the standard library. The repository includes the CI workflow, operator procedures and architecture diagram. [Validation](validation.txt) · [Benchmark](benchmark.json) · [Runbook](../docs/runbook.md) · [Design](../docs/architecture.md)
- **Security, licensing and deployment review.** The local review covered synthetic provenance, clean implementation, parameterized database operations, read-only authenticated loopback access, secret handling and absence of order execution. Organizational IP assignment, provider entitlements and paper/production approvals remain with the owners. Hosted CI requires a connected repository.
