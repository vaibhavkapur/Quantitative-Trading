# Acceptance Checklist & Evidence — Multi-Strategy PMS (`plan.md` §6)

All MVP acceptance criteria from `plan.md` §6 are verified below with concrete automated tests and raw retained evidence.

| # | Acceptance Criterion (`plan.md` §6) | Status | Verification & Attached Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ledger replays to identical balances. | **PASS** | `tests/integration/test_sqlite_store_and_recovery.py`, `scripts/reproduce.py`, `reports/evidence/reproducibility_check.json` (`reproducible_across_clean_builds: true`), `reports/evidence/demo_summary.json` (`recovered_identically: true`). |
| 2 | Fund totals equal strategy totals plus unallocated cash. | **PASS** | `ValuationEngine.value_fund()` enforces `ConsolidationCheck` on NAV, cash, and P&L (`nav_difference_base = 0`, `cash_difference_base = 0`, `pnl_difference_base = 0`); verified in `tests/unit/test_valuation_and_consolidation.py` and `reports/evidence/cod_report.md`. |
| 3 | Every P&L change is explainable by events or marks. | **PASS** | `PnLAttribution` and `compute_pnl_bridge()` enforce `unexplained_residual_base = 0` across trading P&L, unrealized price P&L, FX translation P&L, dividends, fees (including late fees), and audited adjustments; verified in `tests/unit/test_valuation_and_consolidation.py`, `tests/scenarios/test_eight_required_scenarios.py`, and `reports/evidence/cod_report.json` (`unexplained_residual_base: "0.000000"`). |
| 4 | Unpriced assets flagged. | **PASS** | `EQ-UNPRICED-US` flagged with `pricing_status = MISSING_MARK`, `is_priced = false`, `valuation_complete = false`, and explicit `ValuationException`; stale marks flagged as `STALE_MARK`; verified in `test_scenario_7_missing_mark`, `test_stale_mark_is_flagged_as_unpriced`, and `reports/evidence/cod_report.md`. |
| 5 | Reconciliation never silently modifies history. | **PASS** | SQLite `BEFORE UPDATE` / `BEFORE DELETE` triggers block mutations (`ImmutabilityViolationError`), `ReconciliationRun.mutated_history == False`, and break resolutions require `Role.APPROVER` to append new `AdjustmentEvent` records; verified in `test_sqlite_triggers_prevent_history_mutation`, `test_reconciliation_never_mutates_history_and_requires_approver_to_resolve`, and `test_scenario_8_reconciliation_break`. |
| 6 | A clean checkout can run the documented setup, tests and demo with permitted fixtures. | **PASS** | Verified end-to-end via `bash scripts/setup.sh && bash scripts/validate.sh` with synthetic fixtures documented in `data/fixtures/manifest.json` and pinned `uv.lock`. |
| 7 | Architecture, dependency assumptions, known limitations and runbook are committed. | **PASS** | Committed in `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/api_and_schema.md`, `docs/schema.sql`, `docs/runbook.md`, `docs/dependency_and_ip_audit.md`, `docs/unsupported_features.md`, and `docs/adr/ADR-001..003`. |
| 8 | Relevant CI checks pass and raw evaluation or test evidence is retained. | **PASS** | Ruff lint (`All checks passed!` in `reports/evidence/ruff_output.txt`), Pytest (`27 passed in 0.17s` in `reports/evidence/pytest_output.txt` and `reports/evidence/pytest_junit.xml`), COD reports (`cod_report.json`, `cod_report.md`), and demo benchmark (`demo_summary.json`). |
| 9 | Security, data licensing, IP and any deployment approval requirements are reviewed. | **PASS** | Reviewed and documented in `docs/dependency_and_ip_audit.md`; zero secrets in repo, `live_order_execution_enabled = false` enforced by `ControlViolationError` (`HTTP 403`), external GitHub repos not copied pending licensing/IP clearance. |
